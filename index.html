FAQ

Questions clients usually ask first.

How do I book?

Send a project inquiry with placement, size, references, budget, availability, and a clear description.

Where do the MP3s go?

Upload them to assets/audio/ and name them suno-ad-01.mp3, suno-ad-02.mp3, suno-ad-03.mp3, and suno-ad-04.mp3, or update the track list in the script.

Why do some images show neon cards?

That is the built-in fallback. Once the real JPGs exist in assets/, the real images display automatically.
